About SovSwift

SovSwift (Sovereignty + Swift) is a groundbreaking initiative by the Democracy for the Citizens Party (DCP) to reimagine Kenya's foreign policy for the 4th Industrial Revolution.

For 60+ years, Kenya's foreign policy has been conducted in a rarefied atmosphere – remote from the lived experiences of ordinary Kenyans. As the current government's own Prime Cabinet Secretary admitted, the policy appears "alien to Kenyans."

DCP will change this. Our foreign policy will serve every Kenyan citizen, protect our digital sovereignty, and position Kenya as a global leader on our own terms.

Our Policy Vision

The DCP's alternative foreign policy framework for Kenya's future.

The Problem

Kenya's foreign policy suffers from a legitimacy crisis, technological obsolescence, and disconnection from citizens. The current government's approach has been described as "alien to Kenyans" – erratic, inconsistent, and failing to serve the people.

The DCP Solution

A citizen-centered foreign policy grounded in Kenya's Constitution, serving all Kenyans, protecting our digital sovereignty, advancing economic prosperity, and positioning Kenya as a global leader on African terms.

Citizen-Centric Diplomacy

Foreign policy will no longer be conducted in isolation from citizens. Town halls in all 47 counties, translated policy documents, and diaspora engagement.

Digital Sovereignty

Kenya must control its own digital destiny. Protect critical infrastructure, create cybersecurity jobs, and lead Africa in digital governance.

Economic Sovereignty

Trade agreements that benefit Kenyan workers and businesses. Reduce dependency on foreign aid and support Kenyan innovators.

Pan-African Leadership

Kenya as the voice of Africa in global affairs. Champion AU reforms and advocate for fair representation in international institutions.

Constitutional Governance

Foreign policy aligned with Kenya's Constitution. Uphold national values: patriotism, unity, rule of law, and human rights.

Five Pillars of DCP's Foreign Policy

Built on Kenya's Constitution, serving all citizens, advancing our national interests.

Digital Sovereignty

Full control over Kenya's digital infrastructure, data, and cyber destiny – protecting our national interests.

Citizen Diplomacy

Foreign policy that serves every Kenyan – with grassroots engagement, diaspora integration, and local voices.

Economic Justice

Trade, investment, and technology transfer that benefits Kenyan workers, businesses, and communities.

Cyber Resilience

Protecting citizens, businesses, and government from cyber threats – creating jobs and security for Kenyans.

Global Leadership

Kenya as a primary architect of the global order – UNSC seat, AU reform, Nairobi as a global hub for the Global South.
