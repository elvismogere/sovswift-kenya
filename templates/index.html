About SovSwift

SovSwift (Sovereignty + Swift) is a groundbreaking national project that reimagines Kenya's foreign policy for the Fourth Industrial Revolution. It bridges the gap between elite diplomacy and citizens' daily lives, ensuring Kenya becomes a digitally sovereign, economically prosperous, and globally respected nation.

Grounded in the Constitution of Kenya and aligned with the Bottom‑Up Economic Transformation Agenda (BETA), SovSwift offers a practical roadmap for the next government to deliver tangible benefits to every Kenyan.

A project of the Democracy for the Citizens Party (DCP).

Five Pillars of SovSwift

Each pillar is designed to transform Kenya's diplomatic and digital landscape.

Digital Sovereignty

Full control over Kenya's digital infrastructure, data, and cyber destiny by 2030.

Citizen‑Centric Diplomacy

Foreign policy that serves every Kenyan – with grassroots engagement and diaspora integration.

Economic Transformation

Trade, investment, and technology transfer to create jobs and prosperity.

Cyber Resilience

Protecting citizens, businesses, and government from cyber threats.

Global Leadership

Kenya as a primary architect of the global order – UNSC seat, AU reform, Nairobi hub.
